## Corrections applied

Claims in circulation that were wrong, corrected throughout:

1. **RO membrane pore size is ~0.0001 microns, not 0.00001.** The smaller
   figure is off by 10× and would be smaller than a single atom.
2. **SeaPack is discontinued and HTI has closed.** Forward osmosis pouches can
   no longer be recommended without an availability caveat.
3. **Improvised solar stills don't meet survival need.** A few hundred mL/day
   against a ~3 L/day requirement. Presented as a technique, never a plan.

Verify prices and availability before publishing — records are dated
2026-09-08.
