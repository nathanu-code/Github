# Footage permission request (for the team to send; the automation never sends it)

Subject / DM:

> Hi {name}, I'm with World Ignored ({account links}), a page covering natural disasters around the
> world. We'd like to share your video of {event} in {location} on {date} on our accounts (TikTok,
> YouTube Shorts, Instagram, Facebook, X, Threads, LinkedIn), with credit to you as "{credit}" in the
> caption.
>
> Could you confirm:
> 1. You filmed this video yourself (or own the rights to it)?
> 2. You give World Ignored permission to post it, with light edits (cropping to vertical, a title
>    and a safety message), on the platforms above?
> 3. How you'd like to be credited?
>
> A simple "Yes, you have my permission" reply is enough. Thank you, and we hope you and everyone
> nearby are safe.

Save the reply (screenshot or email) in Drive "World Ignored/Sources and Permissions" named
`<item id>_permission.png`, then record it:
`python -m wi update <id> '{"rights": {"status": "cleared", "license_type": "written_permission", ...}}'`

Licensing agencies (paid, fast, clear terms): Storyful, Newsflare, ViralHog, Jukin/Trusted Media,
Reuters Connect, AP Newsroom. Their licence confirmation is the evidence.
