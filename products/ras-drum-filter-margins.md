# RAS Drum Filters — Margin Breakdown

Captured 2026-09-08. Retail comps: [RAU Filter 10 m³/h](https://raufilter.com/product/rau-filter-rotary-drum-filter-10/),
[AquaDream T3 3-ton](https://aquadreamusa.com/products/micro-pond-rotary-drum-filter-3-tons),
[GC Koi FREE Drum Filter 60](https://gckoi.com/products/free-drum-filter-60-15-000-gph),
[Play It Koi RDF range](https://playitkoi.com/collections/rotary-drum-filters-and-sieves),

## Headline

**Far better than grow tents — 13% to 43% net margin depending on channel,
against 2% for tents.** The earlier call holds up, and the numbers show why.

But two things I got wrong or overstated last time, corrected below: **the
low-end arbitrage is already being run on Amazon**, and **this product carries
liability exposure a grow tent does not.**

**Look at the first two rows again.** The AquaDream T3 moves **807 GPH for
$1,399**. The RAU moves **2,600 GPH for $692**. Three times the price for
less than a third of the flow.

**Pricing in this category is not spec-driven.** Buyers can't easily compare
across brands, and the AquaDream's "list $2,436, sale $1,399" anchor — 43% off —
tells you the category tolerates high nominal margins. That is pricing power,
and it's the single most important fact in this analysis.

## Value density — why this beats tents

| Product | Price | Volume | $ per m³ |
|---|---|---|---|
| FREE Drum 60 | $6,200 | 0.311 m³ | **$19,936** |
| AquaDream T3 | $1,399 | 0.085 m³ | **$16,459** |
| RAU Filter 10 | $692 | 0.051 m³ | **$13,569** |
| [Grow tent kit](grow-tents-and-vertical-farming.md) | $350 | 0.300 m³ | **$1,167** |

**12–17× the value per cubic metre of a grow tent kit.** Freight stops being the
thing that eats the deal.

Better still: the RAU's **shipping weight is 11 lbs** for a 10 m³/h unit — these
are food-grade polypropylene housings with a 316L stainless screen, not heavy
steel machines.

**Dimensional weight check:** 21.65 × 12 × 12 in ÷ 139 = **22.4 lb billable**
against 11 lb actual. Still comfortably **parcel-shippable** — UPS/FedEx ground,
not LTL freight and pallets.

That unlocks something bulky goods can't do: **air freight is viable.** At
~$5–8/kg, 20 units × 5 kg is $500–800, roughly the same as LCL ocean once you
add destination charges — but with a **5–7 day lead time instead of 30–45 days.**
That transforms working-capital cycles and lets you restock reactively.

## ⚠ The FOB price is the one number I don't have

The Qihang catalog showed the **PM-10 (10 T/H)**, **PM-100 (100 T/H)**, **YCM
series** and **Bakki showers** with **no prices published**. The only anchor is
the **PP koi pond filter at $291.92** (from $328) at **MOQ 1**, spec'd for
3–20 m³ ponds — roughly comparable to the RAU 10's 20,000 L rating.

An MOQ-1 Alibaba price is effectively retail. A real quote at 20–50 units should
come in materially lower. **Everything below is modelled at $230 FOB
(conservative) with a $180 sensitivity — but you must get a written quote at
your target quantity before treating any of this as real.**

## Landed cost

Modelling a 20-unit order of the 10 m³/h class:

| Line | $230 FOB | $180 FOB |
|---|---|---|
| FOB | $230.00 | $180.00 |
| Freight (1.02 m³ LCL, or air — see above) | $35.00 | $35.00 |
| Customs broker / entry ($150 ÷ 20) | $7.50 | $7.50 |
| **Duty @ 30%** | $69.00 | $54.00 |
| **Landed cost** | **$341.50** | **$276.50** |

**On duty:** the likely classification is **HTS 8421.21** — filtering or
purifying machinery for water — where the MFN base rate may be free, but
**Section 301 applies on top**. I have modelled a flat 30%. Get a broker to
confirm the classification and the current stack in writing; see the
[2026 tariff position](reselling-economics.md), which moved significantly this
year.

Returns are set at 8%, not 5% — this is an electromechanical product that
requires correct plumbing, so buyer-error returns are higher than for passive
goods.

**12.6% is workable but not exciting**, and Amazon is the wrong channel anyway
(see below).

**Direct works here in a way it doesn't for tents**, for two reasons: the buyer

**this category is fully advertisable** — no cannabis-adjacency problem, so
Google, Meta, forums, and YouTube are all open. That's a structural advantage
over the grow tent category.

This is the play the price ladder actually points to. The AquaDream sells
**807 GPH for $1,399**; pricing a 2,600 GPH unit at $1,000 with support, spares,
and a real warranty is *under*-cutting the market, not gouging it.

### Dealer / wholesale

Specialty dealers (Play It Koi, Webb's Water Gardens, GC Koi) buy at roughly
50–55% of MSRP.

| | $230 FOB | $180 FOB |

## The real margin is in spares

The RAU ships with a **spare water level sensor** — which tells you exactly
which part fails.

| Consumable / wear part | Character |
|---|---|
| 316L stainless screens (50–200 µm) | Wear item, sized per application |
| Water level sensors | Known failure part — ships with a spare |
| Gear motors | Wear item |
| Spray nozzles / spray bar | Clogs and wears |
| Seals and gaskets | Routine replacement |

Spares are **tiny, light, high-margin, and repeat.** Screens especially — a
$15 FOB stainless screen sells for $80–150, ships in an envelope, and every
installed unit needs them.

**This is the actual business.** The filter is customer acquisition; the spares
are the profit. It requires an installed base first, which is why the direct and
dealer channels matter more than Amazon — you own the customer relationship in
both.

## What I got wrong last time

**1. The low-end arbitrage is already being run.** There are Chinese-sourced
RDFs on Amazon now — **elelife 3000GPH Basic and 4000GPH Advanced**, and a
**RINOO 4T10-1**. My "no dominant US brand" claim still holds (Evolution Aqua,
FREE Drum, SeaSide Aquatics and AquaDream are all small; there's no
VIVOSUN-equivalent) — but **the budget slot is occupied.**

The opening is **mid-to-premium with support and spares**, not undercutting on
price. Which, given the 43% margin at $1,000, is the better half of the market
anyway.

**2. I understated the liability.** A grow tent that fails is an annoyance. **A
drum filter that jams or overflows floods a fish room and kills the stock.** For
a koi keeper that can be tens of thousands of dollars of fish, and koi owners
know exactly what their fish are worth.

That means: **product liability insurance is mandatory, not optional**, and you
need the capacity to actually service units rather than just ship them.

## The other real risks

**Certification is a genuine cost line.** These have a mains-connected
controller, a gear motor, a water-level sensor, and sometimes a UV lamp —
electrical near water. For US commercial sale you want **UL or ETL listing**.
That is **Tier 2** in the [sourcing framework](sourcing-from-china.md): either
verify the factory's certification is genuine and transferable, or budget for
your own listing. Do not assume a CE mark covers you.

**Installation complexity creates a support burden.** RDFs are gravity-fed and
need correct sump and plumbing design. Either you sell to knowledgeable buyers
or you absorb the support cost — and at 26–43% margin you can afford support, so
this is a reason to price high rather than a reason to avoid the category.

**The market is small.** US koi plus small-scale aquaculture is a niche. That
cuts both ways: little competition, but you will not move thousands of units a
year. Model it as a **hundreds-of-units business with good per-unit margin**,
not a volume play.

## Verdict

| | Grow tents | RAS drum filters |
|---|---|---|
| Amazon margin | **2%** | **12.6%** |
| Direct margin | 3–11% | **26%** |
| Direct @ premium positioning | n/a — commodity | **43%** |
| Value density | $1,167/m³ | **$13,569–19,936/m³** |
| Advertisable normally | **No** | **Yes** |
| Brand competition | Severe | Fragmented |
| Air freight viable | No | **Yes** |
| Liability exposure | Low | **High** |
| Certification needed | Kit only | **Yes — UL/ETL** |
| Recurring revenue | None | **Screens and spares** |

**Proceed, with three conditions:**

1. **Get a written FOB quote** at 20 and 50 units for a specific model. Nothing
   above is real until you have it. The whole model swings on this number.
2. **Get a customs broker's HTS classification** for 8421.21 and the current
   Section 301 stack in writing.
3. **Price and sell direct at $900–1,200 with support and spares** — not on
   Amazon at $692, and not against the elelife listings on price.

**Start with the [$291.92 koi filter at MOQ 1](aquaponics.md).** One unit,
sub-$300, tells you build quality, screen quality, motor noise, and whether the
controller is sane — before you commit to a container of anything.
