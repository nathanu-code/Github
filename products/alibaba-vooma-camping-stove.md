## Unknowns that must be resolved before ordering

These are not nitpicks — each one can make the stove useless or unsafe:

1. **Which canister standard?** This is the critical question. Remote-hose
   stoves connect via one of several incompatible fittings — threaded EN417 /
   Lindal 7/16" (Western backpacking canisters), Korean puncture-type butane,
   or Japanese cassette. **If the fitting doesn't match fuel you can buy
   locally, the stove is scrap.** Ask for the connector standard and a photo of
   the thread.
2. **Fuel type — butane or isobutane blend?** Plain butane stops vaporizing
   around 30 °F / 0 °C. For a cold-weather or year-round kit this alone may
   disqualify it. See [`stoves-and-fuel.md`](stoves-and-fuel.md).
3. **What is in a "set"?** Is the kettle included, or photography only? Is the
   canister cover included? Is a regulator included, and is it fixed or
   adjustable?
4. **Certification.** No CE / CSA / UL / ANSI marking is shown. This is a **gas
   appliance** — the regulator and hose are safety-critical. For resale in the
   US this is a hard blocker; for personal use it is your risk to carry.
5. **BTU / kW output.** Not stated. Without it, "cooks three meals" is
   marketing, not a spec.

## Assessment

At $18–23 landed-ish per unit this is cheap for a remote-canister stove, and the
form factor is genuinely good — a remote canister keeps the fuel away from
burner heat and allows a low, stable pot platform.

But note what it is and isn't:

- **It is** a plausible convenience/car-camping stove and a reasonable candidate
  for a resale line if certification checks out.
- **It is not** a survival-fuel answer. A proprietary or butane-only canister
  system is the *opposite* of what you want for stockpiling — see the fuel
  analysis, where propane wins decisively on shelf life, cost per BTU, and cold
  performance.

**Recommendation:** order a sample (MOQ 10 at $230) only after the supplier
confirms the canister standard in writing and provides certification documents.
Do not scale to the 500-set tier on 30 reviews and a 20% reorder rate.
