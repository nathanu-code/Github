## What it is

A portable, handheld seawater desalination device that turns seawater into fresh
drinking water instantly. It is human-powered — a hand lever driving an energy
recovery device — so it needs no batteries, electricity, or fuel.

Marketed for sailors, campers, travelers, emergency/go-bag kits, and
humanitarian relief.

## Notes / due diligence

- A 3-month warranty is short for a $400 mechanical device, and the consumables
  exclusion means the parts most likely to fail first are not covered.
- No published membrane lifespan makes total cost of ownership hard to model.
- The homepage links an external token/securities offering (ixSwap) and a
  Revolut checkout link alongside the store — worth noting before ordering at
  volume.
- Output "below 1,000 TDS" is at the upper edge of the WHO palatability
  guideline (<600 mg/L preferred, <1,000 mg/L acceptable). Fine for emergency
  use; not a polished-water spec.
